Questions about working with us.

Short answers, no small print.

Call 084 620 4583
What do you need from me to start?

Your logo if you have one, the photos and words you want used, and access to your domain. If you have none of that yet, we work from a call.

How does pricing work?

One once-off design fee, fixed in writing before work starts. 50% to start, 50% on launch. Hosting and management are optional monthly add-ons.

How long does it take?

Silver about 5 working days, Gold 10, Platinum 15, counted from the day we have your content and access. Stores and directories get a date at kickoff.

Who actually builds the site?

The person you speak to. There is no account manager and no handover.

Can I see it before it goes live?

Yes. You review a working version on a private link, and changes happen before launch, not after.

What if I already have a website?

We either rebuild it or take over the one you have. Send the address and we will tell you which makes more sense.

What happens on the day it goes live?

We put it live, point the domain, and check every page on a phone, a tablet and a desktop before telling you it is done.
