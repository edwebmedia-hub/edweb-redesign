The team your neighbours phone first

Small on purpose, registered by principle, and based in Diep River since 2014.

Built on call-backs we never got

Waterline started in 2014 with one bakkie and a simple bet: if the quote is fixed, the work is certified and the site is left clean, the phone keeps ringing on its own. Twelve years and more than four thousand call-outs later, that is still the whole business plan.

We stayed small on purpose. Every plumber on our books is PIRB-registered, carries the same stocked van and works to the same checklist, so the standard does not depend on who you get. No franchise scripts, no commission-chasing upsells, no stranger subcontractors in your home.

Most of our work now comes from two places: neighbours who watched a job happen over the fence, and body corporates tired of paying twice for the same fix. We would rather keep it that way than be the biggest name on a billboard.

The four habits

Not values on a poster. Habits you can check on any job we have ever done.

The quote is the price

Fixed and written before tools come out. Surprises get approved by you, or they do not happen.

Certified, not "sorted"

PIRB registration, SANS-compliant installs and a COC on every certifiable job. Paper you can hand your insurer.

Stocked vans

Geyser valves, common fittings, jetting gear on board. First-visit fixes are policy, not luck.

Leave it cleaner

Shoe covers inside, dust sheets under every cut, and the site swept before the invoice is sent.
