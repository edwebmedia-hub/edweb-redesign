Certificates of compliance

Certificates of Compliance

Selling, transferring or insuring a property means the wiring has to be tested and certified. We inspect, list what needs attention and quote before doing any repair work.

When do I need one?
A valid certificate is required when a property changes hands, and insurers commonly ask for one after an electrical claim. Certificates also need replacing after work that alters the installation.
What if it fails?
You get a written list of what failed and why, with a quote for the repairs. You are free to have that work done by anyone. We only issue the certificate once the installation passes.
Do you certify solar?
Yes. Every installation we complete is certified, and we can inspect and certify systems installed by others once any defects have been corrected.
How long does it take?
A standard house is usually a morning's work, depending on the size of the installation and how accessible the board and circuits are.
