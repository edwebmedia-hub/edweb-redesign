Who you are dealing with

Small team, done properly

Brightline is an electrical contractor working out of Somerset West, doing solar and backup installations for homes and small businesses across the Helderberg.

Why we work this way

Sold hard, often badly

Systems arrived undersized, boards were left unlabelled, and nobody explained what would happen at nine at night in winter.

We built Brightline around the opposite habit. An electrician looks at the property before a price exists. The quote names the equipment. The board gets labelled so the next person can read it. Then we sit with you and explain what the system will carry, and what it will not.

It is slower to sell that way. It is a lot easier to live with afterwards.

On every job

The standards we hold

  • We look before we price

    No quote goes out on a phone call alone. The board, the roof, the meter and your actual usage decide the system, not a package tier.

    On site

  • Named equipment, itemised price

    Every quote lists the make and model of the inverter, batteries and panels. If a component changes, you hear about it before it is installed.

    On paper

  • Work you can inspect

    Cables in trunking, circuits labelled, isolators where they belong. If a job is going to be hidden behind a board cover, it still gets done as if it were on show.

    On the wall

  • The same number answers

    You deal with the people who did the installation. Service calls do not start from scratch with a stranger reading a file.
