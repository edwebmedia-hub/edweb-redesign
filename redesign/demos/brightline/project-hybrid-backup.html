Hybrid home backup

A four-bedroom home in Somerset West that lost everything during an outage, gate and fibre included, now runs its essential circuits off a hybrid inverter and battery.

The brief

Keep the house running

The owners did not want a system that pretended to run everything. They wanted the fridge, the lights, the internet, the gate and one plug in the study to stay alive, and they wanted to know exactly what would happen when the power went.

We measured the property's usage over a week before quoting, then split the board so the essential circuits sit on their own supply. The rest of the house waits for the grid, which keeps the battery smaller, the cost lower and the runtime honest.

What went in

The installed system

Every item was named by make and model on the quote before a cable was pulled. Nothing was substituted on site.

  • Hybrid inverter

    Single-phase hybrid unit wall-mounted in the plant room, configured for grid support with battery priority in the evening peak.

    Plant room

  • Lithium battery bank

    Wall-mounted lithium storage sized against the measured evening load, with room on the rail to add a second module later.

    Expandable

  • Essential-loads board

    Separate board carrying the fridge, lighting circuits, fibre and gate motor, each breaker labelled so the owner can read it.

    Labelled

  • Surge and isolation

    Surge protection on the incoming supply, DC and AC isolators mounted where a technician can reach them without opening the board.

    Safety

  • Monitoring and sign-off

    App set up on the owner's phone, a walkthrough of the limits, and the Certificate of Compliance issued on completion.

    Handover
