No contract is too big or too small for us

That sentence has been on our wall since we started and it still decides which jobs we take.

Our area of expertise is high pressure roof cleaning and painting, general exterior painting, all types of roof waterproofing, and roof repairs and replacements. We also repair and install gutter and fascia systems, and where a leak has already damaged the ceiling below, we bring in the ceiling and cornice work as a second line on the same quote. Over nineteen years that has meant single valley repairs on houses in Kuils River in the same week as a factory roof in Firgrove.

We pride ourselves on being the preferred contractor to numerous property managers, building contractors and architects. All our services are under the total commitment and personal supervision of the owner, which is the part that does not scale and the part we refuse to give up.

When we quote, we come and look

We prefer to visit the site, assess the area, take pictures and formulate a professional, well-detailed report or quotation. That is what lets you make an informed decision instead of trusting an adjective.

  1. The call

    You tell us the address

    Phone 021 555 0173, or reach the owner on 082 555 0173 for quotations. Tell us what you are seeing and roughly when it started.

  2. On the day

    We get onto the roof, or put a platform up

    Accessible areas are walked and inspected. Where access is unsafe or the roof is large, we survey it from a platform instead, at no risk to anybody.

  3. In the quote

    Photographs, not adjectives

    A written report with pictures of each defect, so you can see the cracked valley or the lifted ridging yourself and decide what to approve.

  4. Every day on site

    The owner supervises the work

    A safety officer is on site where the job requires one, and a safety file is compiled per job.

  5. Last hour

    We clean up and walk it with you

    Site left clean, offcuts removed, and the finished work checked with you before the team leaves.

The environment and our staff are valuable to us

We treat both with dignity and respect. In a trade with this much turnover, the fact that the same faces come back every year is the clearest thing we can tell you about how the company is run.

We employ young workers and train them to become excellent workers, giving them a chance at a better future. Safety, health and environmental training is not a box we tick. It is the reason nobody has to be talked into going up.

  • An annual medical is required for all staff
  • Public liability cover, and the certificate goes out with every quote
  • Working at heights training, a strict three-day course, for all roof workers
  • Safety officers on site to supervise
  • A safety file compiled for each job that requires one
