Why our quote takes a day longer

Most roofing quotes are guessed from the driveway. Ours is not. We climb it, photograph it, and write down what we found before we write down a price.

  1. The call

    You tell us the address

    Phone the office on 021 555 0173 or send the address and what you are seeing. There is no charge for the visit and no obligation after it.

  2. On the day

    We get onto the roof

    Every accessible area is walked and inspected, harnessed and anchored. Where access is genuinely unsafe we survey from a platform rather than guess from the ground.

  3. In the quote

    Photographs, not adjectives

    The quote arrives within 48 hours as a written report with a picture of each defect, so you can see the cracked valley or the lifted ridging yourself and decide what to approve.

  4. Every day on site

    A foreman on every job

    One named foreman runs the job start to finish, so you are never explaining yesterday to a stranger. A safety file is compiled per job.

  5. Last hour

    We clean up and walk it with you

    Site left clean, offcuts and old covering removed, and the finished work checked with you before the team leaves.

Roofs that cannot leak.

Homeowners are half our diary. The other half is the buildings where a leak stops something more expensive than a carpet.

  • Body corporates One leak is forty owners' problem and a special levy nobody voted for.
  • Retirement estates Occupied rooms, residents who cannot move out for a week, and no tolerance for noise or mess.
  • Wine cellars & packhouses There is product under that roof. A December leak costs more than the roof did.
  • Schools Six usable weeks a year to do the work in, and the holidays do not move for anyone.
  • Guesthouses Every extra day of scaffolding is a room that cannot be sold.
  • Light industrial Long spans, tired sheeting, and a floor full of stock that cannot get wet.

The line that has been on our office wall since we started is "no contract is too big or too small", and the crew that recoats a packhouse is the crew that comes to a single house in Gordon's Bay.

Trained for heights, insured for the fall

Roofing is the trade where cutting corners hurts someone. Safety training is not paperwork here, it is the reason the same crew comes back every year.

  • Working-at-heights certification for every member of the roof crew
  • Harnesses and anchor points on every pitched job, not just the steep ones
  • Public liability cover, and the certificate goes out with the quote
  • A safety file compiled per job, handed over at the end of it
  • Apprentices trained up in-house rather than hired in and out per job

Before you call

The eight things people ask us most. Still not sure? Phone the office on 021 555 0173 and ask.

Do you charge for a quote? add

No. The site visit, the inspection and the written report are free, and there is no obligation after you receive them.

How quickly can you come out to a leak? add

Phone the office and we will book the soonest slot. In the Cape winter the diary fills fast, so the earlier in the season you call, the better. If water is coming in right now, say so when you phone and we will treat it differently.

Can you patch it, or does the whole roof need doing? add

Usually a repair. A roof that is structurally sound and only failing at the joints does not need replacing, and the report will say so plainly. Where a re-cover genuinely is the cheaper answer over ten years, we will show you why in the photographs rather than just assert it.

Why does the leak show up nowhere near the damp patch? add

Because water runs along battens, purlins and the underside of the covering before it drops. Tracing it is most of the skill, and it is the reason we will not quote off a photograph of your ceiling.

Do you do domestic work or only commercial? add

Both. Body corporates, packhouses and guesthouses sit in the same diary as single houses. No contract is too big or too small.

Which areas do you cover? add

The Helderberg first, where we are based, then out through the Winelands and the eastern side of the metro. For larger contracts we travel further.

Somerset West, Strand, Gordon's Bay, Firgrove, Sir Lowry's Pass, Stellenbosch, Kuils River, Blue Downs, Macassar and Grabouw.

Will you fix the ceiling the leak damaged? add

We close the roof first, then bring in the ceiling and cornice work as a second line on the same quote, so you are not chasing another contractor after we leave.

Who is actually on site? add

One named foreman runs your job from the first day to the last, with the same crew under him. You get his number, and he phones you at the end of each day with what was done.
