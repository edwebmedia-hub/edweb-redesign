What we build

Four things, done properly, rather than everything done adequately. Whichever one you need, the quote is written and itemised, the programme is agreed before we start, and one foreman carries the job to handover.

New builds

Turnkey homes and small developments from a cleared stand to a swept floor. We work to approved plans and engineer detail, and the same site team stays on your job from foundation to final coat.

  • Site establishment, setting out and foundations
  • Structural shell, roof and envelope
  • Plumbing, electrical and finishes co-ordinated in-house
  • Council inspections managed on your behalf
  • Occupation certificate and guarantee file at handover

Renovations & additions

Second storeys, room additions and full interior renovations. Roughly half of these run with the family still living in the house, so containment and programme discipline matter as much as the building work.

  • Structural alterations with engineer sign-off
  • Second-storey additions over occupied homes
  • Kitchen and bathroom renovations
  • Dust sheeting, containment and agreed working hours
  • House left lockable and safe every evening

Civil & groundworks

The work nobody sees and everybody regrets skimping on. Retaining structures, foundations and stormwater built to engineer specification, with test results and certificates filed as we go.

  • Site clearing, bulk earthworks and platform preparation
  • Engineered retaining walls and lateral support
  • Stormwater, drainage and sub-surface work
  • Compaction testing and concrete cube results recorded
  • Structural certification on completion

Maintenance & snagging

Defect lists closed out without being chased, including work another contractor started. We photograph every item, fix it, and sign it off with you rather than reporting it complete and hoping.

  • Snag lists closed out and signed off item by item
  • Structural repairs and crack investigation
  • Waterproofing, damp and roof leak remediation
  • Taking over from a contractor who has walked off site
  • Scheduled maintenance for managed properties

On every job, without asking

These are not extras and they are not line items. They are the reason the programme holds.

  • A written quoteItemised into materials, labour and preliminaries. Valid 30 days.
  • A named foremanOne person accountable for your site, reachable on his mobile.
  • A written programmeMilestone dates agreed before anyone breaks ground.
  • Weekly photographsProgress sent every Friday, whether or not you asked.
  • Approved variations onlyNothing charged that you have not signed off in writing first.
  • A guarantee fileCertificates, warranties and sign-offs in one place at handover.

Not sure which one you need?

Describe the job in a sentence and send a photo. We will tell you what it involves, roughly what it costs, and whether it is worth doing at all.
