Is the website design fee a once-off payment?

Yes. The design fee is a once-off payment. You pay it once and the website is yours. Hosting and management are separate optional monthly add-ons.

Do I need hosting to keep my website live?

Yes. Every website needs hosting to stay live on the internet. Our hosting add-on is R199 per month and includes website hosting and a professional email address. You can also use your own hosting provider if you prefer.

What happens if I do not pay my monthly hosting or management fee?

If a monthly payment is missed, services such as hosting, email access and website updates may be suspended until payment is received. We will always notify you before any action is taken.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. Your once-off design fee covers the full website build. Hosting and management are clearly priced optional add-ons. No unexpected charges.

Can I cancel hosting or management at any time?

Yes. Monthly services can be cancelled at any time by notifying us in writing before the next billing date. No long-term commitments.

How long does it take to build my website?

Most standard websites are completed within a few weeks, depending on the size and how quickly content and feedback are provided. Business builds typically take 5, 10 or 15 working days by plan, confirmed on kickoff and counted from the day content and access arrive.

Will I own the website files once it is built?

Yes. Once the once-off design fee is paid in full, all website files and design work belong to you. Files are handed over on request once all outstanding fees are settled.

How is the design fee paid?

Half to start and half on launch. The website files and the handover follow once the fee is settled in full.
