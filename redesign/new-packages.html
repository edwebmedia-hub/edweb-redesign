Questions about pricing.

How the money works, answered before you ask.

Call 084 620 4583
Is the design fee a once-off payment?

Yes. You pay it once and the website is yours. Hosting and management are separate optional add-ons.

How is the design fee paid?

Half to start and half on launch. The files and the handover follow once the fee is settled in full.

Do I need hosting to keep my website live?

Yes. Ours is R199 a month including a professional email address, or use your own provider.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. The once-off fee covers the full build. Hosting and management are clearly priced add-ons.

Can I cancel hosting or management at any time?

Yes. Cancel in writing before the next billing date. No long-term commitments.

What happens if I miss a monthly payment?

A missed payment can suspend hosting, email and updates until it is settled. We always notify you first.

How long does it take to build my website?

Business builds typically take 5, 10 or 15 working days by plan, counted from the day content and access arrive.

Will I own the website files once it is built?

Yes. Once the fee is paid in full, all files and design work belong to you.
