Before you write

What we will ask you anyway

Include these and the first reply can be a real answer instead of a list of questions.

Extent and title

Total hectares, how many portions or title deeds, and whether the property is registered in a personal name, a trust or a company.

Water

Boreholes and their yields, dams, river frontage, and whether there is a registered water use licence, a general authorisation or an application still pending.

What it currently produces

Crops or livestock, hectares under production, and the last few seasons of yield or carrying capacity if you have them.

Zoning and VAT

Current zoning, any servitudes registered against the title, and whether the seller is a VAT vendor. This changes the price a buyer actually pays.
