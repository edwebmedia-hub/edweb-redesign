An agency built for agricultural property, not adapted to it

ERFDEVCO sells farms, agricultural land, game farms and smallholdings across South Africa. The agency is led by Martiens Du Plessis, an economics graduate of Stellenbosch, registered with the Property Practitioners Regulatory Authority.

Residential portals were built for houses, so they ask about bedrooms. A buyer looking at 400 hectares needs to know the borehole yield, the carrying capacity, the zoning and whether the water use licence is registered or still under application. That is what our listing schedule asks, and it is why our listings read the way they do.

If a fact is not known, the listing says so rather than filling the gap. That is the whole point of publishing the schedule instead of a paragraph.

Six services, all of them agricultural

No residential, no commercial, no rentals. The whole agency points at one kind of property.

Farm sales

Working farms taken on mandate, scheduled, photographed and marketed to buyers who are looking for production, not a weekend house.

Agricultural land sales

Bare land and portions, sold on extent, soil, water and zoning, with the servitudes and title position established up front.

Game farm sales

Certified fencing, species counts, lodges and hunting infrastructure recorded on the schedule so a buyer knows what is standing and what is running.

Property marketing

Every mandate goes onto erfdevco.com and onto Property24, with matching photography and the same schedule of facts in both places.

Buyer and seller assistance

Guidance through offer, suspensive conditions, VAT treatment and transfer, with the conveyancer and the bank kept on the same set of facts.

Agricultural property consulting

Advice on how a property is likely to be received by the market before it is listed, including what to measure and what to fix first.
