Six services, all of them agricultural

No residential, no commercial, no rentals. The whole agency points at one kind of property.

Farm sales

Working farms taken on mandate, scheduled, photographed and marketed to buyers who are looking for production, not a weekend house.

Agricultural land sales

Bare land and portions, sold on extent, soil, water and zoning, with the servitudes and title position established up front.

Game farm sales

Certified fencing, species counts, lodges and hunting infrastructure recorded on the schedule so a buyer knows what is standing and what is running.

Property marketing

Photography, the written schedule and the marketing copy are produced once and used everywhere the farm is advertised, so a buyer comparing two sources never finds a discrepancy.

Buyer and seller assistance

Guidance through offer, suspensive conditions, VAT treatment and transfer, with the conveyancer and the bank kept on the same set of facts.

Agricultural property consulting

Advice on how a property is likely to be received by the market before it is listed, including what to measure and what to fix first.
