Privacy and POPIA notice

What we collect when you use this site, why we hold it, who sees it, and how to have it removed. Written in plain terms because a notice nobody reads protects nobody.

The notice, section by section

Who is responsible

ERFDEVCO, a property practitioner registered with the Property Practitioners Regulatory Authority, operating from Kleinbaai in the Western Cape. Martiens Du Plessis is the Information Officer for the purposes of the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013. Reach him on 082 900 5019 or at martiens@erfdevco.com.

What this site collects

Only what you type into a form. The enquiry form on a farm page and the contact form both collect your name, email address, phone number if you give one, the province and reason you select, and the message you write. Nothing else about you is gathered: this site sets no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking cookies.

Why we hold it and what we do with it

To answer you, and to keep a record of the enquiry as a property practitioner is required to. A message is emailed to the ERFDEVCO mailbox and read by Martiens. It is not sold, rented, added to a marketing list or passed to a third party, other than the email host that carries the message and any conveyancer or bank you later ask us to deal with on your behalf.

What stays on your own device

Your shortlist and your comparison selection are stored in your browser under erfdevco:shortlist and erfdevco:compare. They never reach us and never leave the device. Clearing your browser data removes them, and nothing on the site depends on them.

What loads from elsewhere

Typefaces are served by Google Fonts, so Google receives the request that fetches them, including your IP address. That is the only third party this site contacts. Photographs, the map and all listing data are served from erfdevco.com.

How long we keep it

An enquiry that does not lead anywhere is kept for two years and then deleted. Where an enquiry becomes a mandate or a sale, the record is kept for five years from the end of that transaction, which is the period the Financial Intelligence Centre Act requires of a property practitioner.

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, and we will act within a reasonable time and tell you when it is done. Ask on 082 900 5019 or at martiens@erfdevco.com. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at complaints.IR@justice.gov.za.

Changes to this notice

If what we collect or what we do with it changes, this page changes with it and the date below is updated. There is no version buried elsewhere.
